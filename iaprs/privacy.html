iAPRS Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · 中文版本

iAPRS is a receive-only APRS client for iPhone and iPad. It decodes amateur-radio packets from the audio of a radio connected to, or held near, the device's microphone, and shows the stations it hears on a map and in a list. iAPRS collects no personal data, has no accounts, and sends nothing about you anywhere.

Microphone

When you turn listening on, iAPRS reads the audio input to decode APRS packets. The audio is processed on the device as it arrives and is never recorded, stored or transmitted. Listening continues in the background only while you have turned it on.

Location

If you allow it, iAPRS uses your location to work out how far away each station is and in which direction. Your location is used on the device only; it is not stored beyond your settings, not shared, and not transmitted. You may instead enter a position by hand, or give none, in which case no distances are shown.

Data kept on your device

The stations, messages, objects, tracks and weather readings iAPRS decodes are saved in the app's own storage on your device, so they remain after a restart. Tracks and weather history are kept for 24 hours. You can delete any station, or everything, from within the app; deleting the app removes it all. This data is received over public amateur radio and never leaves your device.

Network

The only network use is downloading map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) so the map can be drawn. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address and the map area being viewed to that service; see openfreemap.org. iAPRS sends no identifiers, location or usage data with these requests.

What iAPRS does not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data, and no transmission of your callsign, position or packets. iAPRS does not transmit on the radio.

Children

iAPRS collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: github.com/nasacj.

iAPRS 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日

iAPRS 是一个只接收的 APRS 客户端，适用于 iPhone 和 iPad。它从接在（或放在）手机麦克风旁边的电台的声音里解码业余无线电数据包，并把收到的台站显示在地图和列表上。iAPRS 不收集任何个人数据，没有账号，也不会把任何关于你的信息发送到任何地方。

麦克风

当你开启监听时，iAPRS 读取音频输入来解码 APRS 数据包。音频在手机上实时处理，不会被录制、保存或发送。只有在你开启监听期间，App 才会在后台继续监听。

位置

如果你允许，iAPRS 会使用你的位置来计算每个台站的距离和方位。位置只在手机上使用，除了你的设置之外不会保存，不会共享，也不会发送。你也可以手动输入位置，或者不提供位置，此时不显示距离。

保存在手机上的数据

iAPRS 解码得到的台站、消息、物体、轨迹和气象读数，保存在 App 自己的存储空间里，重启后仍然保留。轨迹和气象历史保留 24 小时。你可以在 App 内删除任意台站或全部数据；删除 App 会清除所有数据。这些数据来自公开的业余无线电广播，不会离开你的手机。

网络

唯一的网络访问是从 OpenFreeMap（tiles.openfreemap.org）下载地图瓦片来绘制地图。与所有网络请求一样，该服务会看到你的 IP 地址和所查看的地图区域；详见 openfreemap.org。iAPRS 不会随这些请求发送任何标识、位置或使用数据。

iAPRS 不做的事

没有统计分析，没有广告，没有跟踪，没有收集数据的第三方 SDK，也不会发送你的呼号、位置或数据包。iAPRS 不会通过电台发射。

儿童

iAPRS 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更与联系

如本政策有变更，新版本会发布在本页并更新生效日期。如有问题：github.com/nasacj。
